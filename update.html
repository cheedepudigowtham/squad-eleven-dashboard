For whoever keeps the dashboard up to date. Back to the dashboard

One time: drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar.

Squad Eleven Update

Can't see the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac). If you get a new version of the button, delete the old bookmark first.

After each match:
  1. Open cricclubs.com/MichCA (any CricClubs page works).
  2. Click Squad Eleven Update in your bookmarks bar. A box shows progress, then squad-data.json downloads.
  3. Click Open GitHub upload in that box, drag in squad-data.json and click Commit changes.
  4. About a minute later, refresh the dashboard.
